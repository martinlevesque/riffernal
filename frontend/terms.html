Riffernal is a MIDI practice game: you import your own MIDI files and practice them against your computer keyboard or MIDI controller.

Accounts

Access is by username and password. You are responsible for the activity that happens under your account.

Your songs

You keep the rights to the MIDI files you import. Only upload files you have the right to use and share.

Community

Songs you publish to the community are visible to every user, with your username attached. You can unpublish or delete them at any time.
